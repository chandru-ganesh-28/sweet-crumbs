A home kitchen that grew up

Sweet Crumbs started in 2015 with one oven and a notebook of family recipes. Every birthday we baked for a neighbour turned into three more orders.

Today we still bake in small batches. We use fresh cream, real butter and Belgian chocolate, and our decorators finish every cake by hand, never from a mould.
